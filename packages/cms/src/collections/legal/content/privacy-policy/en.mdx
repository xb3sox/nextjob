This policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use the product. It is a starting point — replace it with counsel-reviewed language before you go live.

## Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and account details, along with technical data like device type, browser, and approximate location.

- **Account data**: name, email, authentication identifiers
- **Usage data**: pages viewed, feature usage, diagnostics
- **Billing data**: handled by our payment provider; we store the minimum needed to manage your subscription

## How we use information

We use this information to operate the product, keep it secure, communicate with you, and improve the experience. We do not sell personal information.

> If you have questions about a specific processing activity, contact us and we will point you to the relevant section.

## Sharing

We share information with vendors who help us run the product (hosting, analytics, email, payments) under contracts that limit how they can use it. We may also share information if required by law.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing.

1. Open your account settings and update what you can directly.
2. Email us for requests we cannot fulfill in the product.
3. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

## Contact

If you have a privacy request, email the address listed on the contact page.
