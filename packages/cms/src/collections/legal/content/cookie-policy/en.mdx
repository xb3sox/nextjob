## What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device. They help the product remember preferences, keep you signed in, and understand how the product is used.

## Types we use

| Type        | Purpose                           | Duration               |
| ----------- | --------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Essential   | Sign-in, security, load balancing | Session or short-lived |
| Preferences | Theme, locale, consent choices    | Up to 12 months        |
| Analytics   | Understand feature usage          | Up to 12 months        |

Essential cookies are required for the product to work. Preference and analytics cookies are used only when you allow them.

## How to manage cookies

You can change your mind at any time from the cookie banner or your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may prevent parts of the product from working.

> Browser controls vary. Look for a privacy or cookies section in your browser settings.

## Updates

We may update this policy when our practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page.
