# NextJob Copywriting Guidelines

> **FR-CONT-02 Specification**  
> Editorial standards for product messaging, landing surfaces, notifications, and candidate communications.  
> **Core Pillars:** Conversational · Confident · Trustworthy · Jargon-Free · Active Voice · < 20 Words / Sentence

---

## 1. Tone of Voice Principles

NextJob speaks like a senior engineer and trusted career partner. We never sound like a spammy recruiter, a growth-hacked bot, or an academic treatise.

| Pillar | How it Sounds | What it Avoids |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Conversational** | Direct, natural, and respectful peer-to-peer dialogue. | Stiff corporate jargon or forced casual banter. |
| **Confident** | Clear, authoritative, and grounded in technical facts. | Apologetic hedging or inflated superlative claims. |
| **Trustworthy** | Radical honesty about capabilities, limits, and security. | Vague promises, "magic" AI claims, or undisclosed automation. |

---

## 2. Vocabulary & Plain Language Standards

We believe clarity builds trust. Replace empty recruitment buzzwords with precise, descriptive language.

### Banned Buzzwords & Preferred Alternatives

| Banned Buzzword / Jargon | Preferred NextJob Alternative | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Revolutionary / Game-changing* | Proven / Verified / Deterministic | Speak through measurable outcomes, not hype. |
| *AI Magic / Wizardry* | Model-driven / Automated / Rule-based | AI is a tool, not magic. Demystify how it works. |
| *Effortless / 1-Click job offer* | Streamlined / Time-saving / Direct | Job searching requires effort; we automate the repetitive parts. |
| *Leverage / Synergy* | Use / Combine / Connect | Plain English communicates higher intelligence. |
| *Guaranteed interview / Job offer* | 3.2x callback lift / High-signal application | Never make promises NextJob cannot guarantee. |
| *Resume blast / Shotgun apply* | Targeted submission / Verified matching | We reject indiscriminate spamming. |
| *Ninja / Rockstar / Guru* | Experienced / Senior / Qualified engineer | Respect professional identities. |

## 3. Structural Rules & Mechanics

### Rule 1: Keep Sentences Under 20 Words
Long, winding sentences hide ambiguity. Keep each sentence under 20 words to maximize reading retention on web and mobile devices.

- **Bad (32 words):** NextJob leverages state-of-the-art artificial intelligence models in order to ensure that all candidate resumes are dynamically adapted and optimized based on the exact qualifications specified in external job postings without manual intervention.
- **Good (15 words):** NextJob tailors resume bullets to match job qualifications using verified claims from your career profile.

### Rule 2: Active Voice Over Passive Voice
Put the actor first. Active sentences are clearer, faster to read, and more accountable.

- **Passive:** Applications are automatically submitted by our connector worker once eligibility is verified.
- **Active:** Our connector worker submits your application as soon as you verify eligibility.

### Rule 3: Complete Sentence Headlines
Per our design system (`DESIGN.md`), headlines must be complete sentences with punctuation that make a distinct claim, not vague two-word fragments.

- **Fragment:** Automated Job Search
- **Complete Sentence:** Honest career automation, verified by evidence.

### Rule 4: Action-Oriented Buttons and CTAs
Buttons must state the exact action and outcome. Never use ambiguous words like "Submit", "Continue", or "Click Here".

- **Avoid:** Submit, Click Here, Go, Learn More.
- **Use:** `Start free — create your account`, `Verify your profile`, `Download submission receipt`.

## 4. Before / After Examples for Each Principle

### Principle A: Conversational
- **Before:** Prospective applicants are required to input authenticated email credentials prior to waitlist accession.
- **After:** Enter your work email below to reserve your early access spot.

### Principle B: Confident
- **Before:** We think our tool might help you possibly get a few more responses from some recruiters.
- **After:** NextJob cuts application prep to 3 minutes and delivers a 3.2x lift in interview callbacks.

### Principle C: Trustworthy
- **Before:** Our proprietary neural algorithm creates the perfect candidate persona for every role.
- **After:** Every generated bullet point links to an approved CareerClaim with verifiable evidence.

### Principle D: Sentence Structure (<20 words)
- **Before:** Because applicant tracking systems reject candidates who fail hard constraint checks, our eligibility engine runs before matching to save your time. (21 words)
- **After:** Our eligibility engine screens hard constraints first so you never waste time on auto-rejects. (15 words)

### Principle E: Active Voice
- **Before:** Cryptographic receipts are generated and signed with SHA-256 by our system for every completed application.
- **After:** NextJob signs every application with SHA-256 to generate an immutable submission receipt.

## 5. Error Messages & Microcopy

1. **Never blame the user:** Avoid "You entered invalid data" or "User error".
2. **State what happened:** "That email address is missing a domain (e.g., name@company.com)."
3. **Offer the exact fix:** "Check for typos or try signing in with your existing account."
4. **Transparent boundaries:** If an ATS connector fails or times out, say so directly and provide the signed receipt.
