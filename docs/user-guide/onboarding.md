# Onboarding User Guide

Setting up your NextJob profile is designed to establish your grounded Career Graph and preferences.

## 1. Account Creation & Sign In

- **Email & Password:** Register with your email address and a secure password.
- **OAuth 2.0:** Alternatively, sign in using Google or GitHub authentication.
- **Tenant Isolation:** Your data is isolated to your individual user tenant account.

## 2. Profile Import & Parsing

- **Supported Upload Formats:** Upload existing CV or resume files in PDF or DOCX format (up to 10 MB).
- **AI Extraction:** NextJob automatically extracts structured claims including employment history, education, certifications, skills, achievements, languages, and contact details.

## 3. Claim Verification

Extracted items are staged as unverified claims requiring your explicit verification:

- **Confirm:** Validate that an extracted claim is accurate. Confirmed claims become verified evidence for job tailoring.
- **Edit:** Correct extracted dates, titles, descriptions, or company names. Editing creates a new versioned claim record.
- **Remove:** Delete claims that are inaccurate or irrelevant.
- **Mark Unverified:** Flag items for later review. Unverified claims will not be used in evidence-constrained tailoring.

## 4. Preferences Capture

Set your job target parameters:

- **Target Roles & Seniority:** Specify desired titles, role families, and seniority levels.
- **Work Mode & Location:** Select remote, hybrid, or on-site preferences along with target locations.
- **Compensation & Logistics:** Define salary expectations, currency, relocation willingness, and notice period.

## 5. Automation Policy Configuration

Select your preferred application automation mode:

- **Manual Mode:** Receive recommendations and tailored assets, but perform all form fills manually.
- **Copilot Mode:** NextJob autofills forms up to Medium risk, but prompts for approval on High-risk or custom fields.
- **Autopilot Mode:** NextJob automatically submits applications when fit, eligibility, and evidence coverage thresholds (≥80%) are met, while still pausing for any Sensitive fields.
